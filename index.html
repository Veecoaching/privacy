Privacy Policy

Vee Coach · Last updated: 15 October 2026

This policy explains what personal information Vee Coach collects when you apply for or use our online coaching, how we use it, and the choices you have. "We", "us" and "Vee Coach" mean Revive (Vee Coach), United Arab Emirates.

1. What we collect

  • When you apply through our Facebook or Instagram form: your name, phone number, email address, and your answers to our questions (your goal, what has got in the way before, when you'd like to start, how ready you are, and your budget preference).
  • When you message us or book a call: the messages you send on WhatsApp and the call time you book.
  • If you become a client: information you share so we can coach you, such as training logs, body measurements, progress photos, food and sleep habits, and any injuries or health information you choose to tell us.

We do not ask for health information in the application form. Please only share it once you're a client and only what you're comfortable with.

2. How we use it

  • To review your application and contact you about coaching by WhatsApp, phone or email.
  • To schedule and hold your consultation call.
  • If you become a client: to design your programme, review your check-ins and coach you.
  • To understand which of our ads bring the right people, using totals rather than individual details.

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not use it to send marketing for other businesses.

3. Who we share it with

We only use services needed to run the coaching, and each has its own privacy policy:

  • Meta (Facebook, Instagram) – runs our ads and collects application forms.
  • WhatsApp – messaging with applicants and clients.
  • 1FIT – the app where clients receive their programme and log progress.
  • Calendly – call booking.
  • Revolut – payments, for clients only. We never see or store your full card details.

Application details are also kept in a private lead list on our own computer. We may disclose information if the law requires it.

4. How long we keep it

  • Applicants who don't become clients: up to 12 months after your last contact with us, then deleted.
  • Clients: for as long as you're coaching with us, plus up to 2 years for records and accounting obligations, unless the law requires longer.

5. Your choices and rights

  • Ask what information we hold about you, or ask us to correct or delete it.
  • Tell us to stop contacting you at any time. Reply "stop" on WhatsApp or email us.
  • Depending on where you live (for example the EU, UK or California), you may have further rights, including the right to complain to your local data protection authority.

To make a request, email veecoaches@gmail.com. We'll reply within 30 days.

6. Security

We use reputable services, keep our devices password-protected, and only access your information to coach you. No online service is completely secure, so please don't send anything you'd be uncomfortable sharing.

7. Age

Our coaching is for adults aged 18 and over. We don't knowingly collect information from anyone under 18.

8. Changes

If we change this policy, we'll update the date at the top of this page.
